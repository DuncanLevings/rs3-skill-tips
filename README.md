# Skill Tips

Skill Tips shows a short, useful tip at the moment you can use it while you train. For example, when mining fills your backpack and you don't own an ore box yet, a card slides in telling you what an ore box does and how to make one.

It's for players who want to train smarter without reading guides: the useful items, the good places to train at your level, and the quests worth doing for what they unlock.

## What you'll get tips about

Tips cover 20 skills: Agility, Archaeology, Construction, Cooking, Crafting, Divination, Farming, Firemaking, Fishing, Fletching, Herblore, Hunter, Invention, Mining, Prayer, Runecrafting, Smithing, Summoning, Thieving and Woodcutting.

- **Useful items and outfits.** Tools, storage, skilling outfits and other items that make training faster or easier, such as the ore box, the wood box or the lumberjack outfit. Each tip says why the item helps and how to get it.
- **Training methods for your level.** As you level up, you'll hear about the main way to train for your new level range, with where to go and what you need.
- **Alternative methods.** Other worthwhile ways to train at your level, for when you want a change of pace.
- **Quality of life.** Unlocks, shortcuts and features that save time across your training.
- **Quests worth doing.** Quests with rewards that make a real difference, such as fairy rings, the Lunar spellbook or Prifddinas. These tips appear once you have the skill levels. For some big unlocks, they appear when you're only a few levels short, with a list of the levels you still need.

Tips never suggest consumables, and they skip things you probably know already.

## When tips appear

- **Item and method tips** appear while you're training the skill. Experience from lamps, books or quest rewards doesn't count as training, so those won't make a tip appear by themselves.
- **Quest tips** wait for a quiet moment, when you've been idle for a few seconds.
- **After combat** the plugin stays quiet for a minute, so tips never pop up mid-fight.
- **Pacing:** by default there are at least 10 minutes between tips and no more than 6 per session. Both can be changed.
- **Starting out:** the first time you use Skill Tips on a character, it skips training methods for levels you've already reached. You'll only hear about the next ones as you get there. Item, quality-of-life and quest tips can appear straight away.

## Tips you already know

Skill Tips skips a tip when it can tell you already know it:

- **Items:** once the plugin has seen you own the item (in your backpack, worn, or in your bank), that tip won't appear again. This stays true if you later store the item in your house or destroy it. For outfits, any single piece counts.
- **Quests:** quest tips stop once you've finished the quest.

**Open your bank once after installing,** so the plugin can see what you already own. Until then it holds back tips about items.

## The tip card

A small card slides in from the edge of the screen:

- **Pin** (top right) saves the tip to your **Pinned tips** list.
- **Dismiss** hides that tip for good.
- **Later** puts it aside for a while: 3 hours of play by default, and you can choose anywhere from 0 to 8 hours. Time only counts while the plugin is running. After that, the tip can come back the next time it fits what you're doing.

Drag the card wherever you like and resize it. The next card appears in the same place.

## Pinned tips and Recent tips

Click the Skill Tips icon in the plugin ribbon to open:

- **Pinned tips:** tips you've pinned, to keep beside your game while you work towards them. Use the red cross to unpin one.
- **Recent tips:** your last 10 tips, so you can pin one you closed by mistake.

You can also set a keyboard shortcut for Pinned tips in the settings.

## Settings

Open the settings from the Plugin Hub. You can:

- turn tips on or off
- choose which kinds of tips you want: items, training methods, quality of life and quests
- choose early, mid or late game tips, and whether to include members tips
- set the time between tips and the number of tips per session
- set how long the plugin stays quiet after combat
- set how long Later pauses a tip
- mute skills you don't want tips for
- turn the card animation off
- reset the card's position

## What the plugin stores

On your computer only, separately for each character:

- which tips you pinned, dismissed, saw recently or already know from owning the item
- whether you own the specific items tips mention, never the rest of your bank
- your skill levels when you first used the plugin, so it knows which training methods are new to you

Your settings and card position are saved too. Skill Tips needs no permissions: it doesn't use the internet or read your chat.
