Third party notices for Skill Tips
======================================

PrettyUI
--------
UI library by j3.gg, loaded as a Plugin Hub dependency (mod.io 6398181). It isn't bundled with
this plugin.

RuneScape Wiki
--------------
Tip text will be written from information on the RuneScape Wiki (https://runescape.wiki),
reworded rather than copied. Wiki content is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/). Each tip names the wiki page it is based on.

LuaUnit (tests only)
--------------------
The test suite uses LuaUnit (BSD license), installed separately on the developer's Lua path.
It isn't shipped with the plugin.
